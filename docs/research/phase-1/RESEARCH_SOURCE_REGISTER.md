## 1. Governance and Source Verification Policy

In accordance with Section 4 and Section 10.3 of the Master Research Specification, this register records all scientific literature, peer-reviewed conference publications, official repository documentation, and benchmark datasets evaluated during Phase 1 research.

Every entry has been audited against the following evidentiary standards:
1. **Zero Hallucination Guarantee:** No synthetic, fabricated, or speculative DOIs, authors, or publication titles are permitted.
2. **Accessible Provenance:** Every citation includes an active, official publisher URL, institutional repository, or official code release.
3. **Evidence Status Criteria:**
   - `VERIFIED`: The publication, dataset documentation, or source code was retrieved and reviewed directly from an authoritative repository (IEEE, ACM, Springer, arXiv, GitHub official organization, or institutional site).
   - `PARTIAL`: Core algorithmic details and peer-reviewed summaries are verified, but proprietary access terms or closed-source model checkpoints require further developer testing in Phase 1.12–1.13.
   - `UNSUITABLE`: Evaluated and formally rejected for EON runtime due to commercial license incompatibility, excessive compute requirements, or safety risks.
