## 1. Executive Summary

This report delivers a thorough computer vision and affective computing investigation into facial expression analysis for OVIC EON. Facial analysis represents the primary visual modality for interpreting human affective signals in human-robot interaction (HRI).

Key findings and architectural decisions include:
1. **Separation of Muscular Observation from Emotional Inference:** In accordance with FACS (Ekman & Friesen, 1978 [SRC-009]) and Barrett et al. (2019 [SRC-005]), EON strictly dissociates anatomical facial muscle movements (Action Units) from subjective emotional inferences. The vision pipeline first detects physical **Action Units (AUs)** and **geometric landmarks** as objective observations before projecting these features into continuous VAD coordinates.
2. **Recommended Vision Pipeline:** The optimal edge-CPU vision architecture consists of:
   - **Detector:** SCRFD-500M (Sample and Computation Redistribution for Efficient Face Detection [SRC-012]) running in $<3\text{ms}$ on CPU.
   - **Landmark Engine:** MediaPipe Face Mesh (Lugaresi et al., 2019 [SRC-010]) generating 468 dense 3D facial landmarks in $8\text{--}12\text{ms}$ on CPU.
   - **AU Regressor:** A lightweight MLP/MobileNetV3 head regressing 12 key Action Units directly from normalized 3D landmark mesh distances in $<2\text{ms}$.
3. **Total Pipeline Latency:** The end-to-end facial perception stack executes within **$13\text{--}17\text{ms}$** on a standard 4-core CPU ($>60\text{ fps}$ throughput), requiring only $\sim 45\text{MB}$ of resident RAM.
4. **Privacy-Preserving Ephemeral Tracking:** EON strictly forbids persistent biometric identification or face embedding storage. Faces are tracked across frames via transient spatial bounding boxes (ByteTrack [SRC-043]) using ephemeral IDs that expire immediately when the subject leaves sensor field-of-view.
5. **Commercial Licensing Safeguard:** Widely cited academic facial datasets (AffectNet, RAF-DB, CK+, DISFA) carry strict non-commercial academic licenses. OpenFace 2.0 carries a restrictive non-commercial license. EON must avoid OpenFace code and deploy a clean-room pipeline leveraging Apache 2.0 / MIT components (MediaPipe, SCRFD, ONNX Runtime).

## 2. Facial Analysis Foundations

### 2.1 Anatomical Basis of Facial Movements
Human facial expressions are produced by the coordinated contractions of superficial facial mimetic muscles innervated by Cranial Nerve VII (the facial nerve). Unlike skeletal muscles that attach bone to bone, facial muscles insert directly into the dermis and connective facial fascia, modulating skin folds, wrinkles, and aperture shapes around the eyes, nose, and mouth.

### 2.2 Objective Observations vs. Emotional Inferences
A fundamental defect in legacy facial emotion recognition software is the immediate, ungrounded classification of a visual frame into an emotional state (e.g., detecting raised lip corners and directly asserting "Happiness = 0.95").

Affective science demonstrates that smiling can indicate:
- True hedonic pleasure (Duchenne smile: AU6 + AU12).
- Social politeness or appeasement (Non-Duchenne smile: AU12 without AU6).
- Embarrassment, nervousness, or masking (AU12 combined with AU15 or averted gaze).

EON resolves this by enforcing a two-stage perception contract:
1. **Level 1 (Sensory Observation):** Objective physical coordinates and Action Unit intensities (e.g., `AU12_intensity: 0.82`, `AU6_intensity: 0.15`, `pitch: -4.2 deg`).
2. **Level 2 (Affective Interpretation):** Probabilistic projection to continuous VAD space with explicit confidence and uncertainty metadata.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    RGB["RGB Video Stream\n(Robot Camera)"] --> Detect["SCRFD-500M Face Detector\n(Bounding Box + 5 Keypoints)"]
    Detect --> Track["ByteTrack Tracker\n(Ephemeral Spatial ID)"]
    Track --> Mesh["MediaPipe Face Mesh\n(468 3D Surface Landmarks)"]
    Mesh --> AU["Geometric AU Regressor\n(12 Action Unit Intensities)"]
    AU --> VAD["Calibrated VAD Projection\n(Continuous Valence, Arousal, Dominance)"]
    VAD --> Bus["Core Event Bus\n('observation.face' Event)"]
```

## 3. Facial Landmark Detection

A comparative evaluation of landmark detection methodologies:

| Method | Landmark Count | Coordinate Space | CPU Latency (Intel/ARM 4-Core) | Robustness to Head Pose | Occlusion Resistance | Commercial License | Technical Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dlib iBUG** | 68 points | 2D Pixel | $28\text{--}42\text{ms}$ | Poor ($<30^\circ$) | Poor | Boost 1.0 | Obsolete; fails with masks or turned heads. |
| **MediaPipe Face Mesh** (Lugaresi et al., 2019 [SRC-010]) | 468 points (+10 iris) | 3D Metric Depth | **$8\text{--}12\text{ms}$** | High (up to $\pm 50^\circ$) | Moderate to High | **Apache 2.0** | **RECOMMENDED.** Dense 3D surface mesh with low CPU load. |
| **RetinaFace-ResNet50** (Deng et al., 2020 [SRC-011]) | 5 keypoints | 2D Pixel | $65\text{--}90\text{ms}$ | Very High ($>75^\circ$) | Very High | MIT | Excellent for detection/alignment; too sparse for AU analysis. |
| **SCRFD-500M** (Guo et al., 2021 [SRC-012]) | 5 keypoints + BBox | 2D Pixel | **$2.5\text{--}4.0\text{ms}$** | High ($>60^\circ$) | High | **Apache 2.0** | **RECOMMENDED.** Ultra-fast front-end detector for MediaPipe ROI cropping. |

MediaPipe Face Mesh achieves landmark depth estimation without a physical depth sensor by leveraging weak perspective camera models calibrated on 3D synthetic face scans, enabling 3D head pose estimation (Yaw, Pitch, Roll) directly on edge CPUs.

## 4. Facial Action Coding System (FACS)

Ekman and Friesen's (1978 [SRC-009]) FACS defines anatomically verified Action Units (AUs). EON selects a primary subset of 12 core Action Units essential for social and emotional robotics:

| Action Unit | Anatomical Description | Muscular Basis | Characteristic Affective Correlates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AU1** | Inner Brow Raiser | *Frontalis, pars medialis* | Sadness, Fear, Distress, Surprise |
| **AU2** | Outer Brow Raiser | *Frontalis, pars lateralis* | Surprise, Questioning, Attention |
| **AU4** | Brow Lowerer | *Corrugator supercilii, Depressor supercilii* | Anger, Frustration, Confusion, Concentration |
| **AU5** | Upper Lid Raiser | *Levator palpebrae superioris* | Fear, Surprise, Alertness |
| **AU6** | Cheek Raiser | *Orbicularis oculi, pars orbitalis* | Duchenne (genuine) Joy, Pain |
| **AU7** | Lid Tightener | *Orbicularis oculi, pars palpebralis* | Anger, Suspicion, Intense Focus |
| **AU9** | Nose Wrinkler | *Levator labii superioris alaeque nasi* | Disgust, Contempt |
| **AU12** | Lip Corner Puller | *Zygomaticus major* | Smiles, Joy, Politeness, Social Affiliation |
| **AU15** | Lip Corner Depressor | *Depressor anguli oris* | Sadness, Disappointment, Grief |
| **AU17** | Chin Raiser | *Mentalis* | Sadness, Doubt, Resistance |
| **AU25** | Lips Part | *Depressor labii inferioris / Relaxation* | Speech, Surprise, Relaxed state |
| **AU45** | Blink / Eye Closure | *Orbicularis oculi* | Fatigue, Stress, Cognitive Load |

### 4.1 Clean-Room Geometric AU Regression
To avoid using proprietary or non-commercially licensed AU models (such as OpenFace), EON regresses AU intensities ($[0.0, 1.0]$) directly from normalized Euclidean and geodesic distances between specific MediaPipe 3D mesh vertices:
- *AU12 (Lip Corner Puller):* Ratio of distance between mesh vertices 61 (left corner) and 291 (right corner) relative to inter-pupillary distance.
- *AU4 (Brow Lowerer):* Distance between vertex 70 (left inner brow) and vertex 300 (right inner brow) combined with vertical displacement to nasal bridge vertex 168.
- *AU6 (Cheek Raiser / Duchenne marker):* Vertical distance between infraorbital ridge vertices and eye aperture landmarks.

## 5. Face Detection and Tracking Without Persistent Biometrics

Robotics deployed in shared public or domestic spaces must comply with strict privacy regulations (GDPR Article 9, EU AI Act). EON implements **zero biometric template persistence**.

### 5.1 Tracking Architecture
1. **Front-End Detection:** SCRFD-500M scans camera frames, producing bounding boxes $[x, y, w, h]$ and confidence scores.
2. **Spatial Association (ByteTrack [SRC-043]):** Uses Kalman filtering and Hungarian matching on bounding box bounding coordinates and motion velocity.
3. **Tracking ID Lifecycle:**
   - On human entry: Assigns random UUID v4 `track_id` (e.g., `trk_9b1deb4d`).
   - Active tracking: Maintained continuously while bounding box is detected in camera field-of-view.
   - On occlusion / exit: Maintained for a timeout buffer of $2.0\text{ seconds}$ (to bridge temporary obstruction behind a pillar or robot arm).
   - On expiration: Tracking ID is permanently deleted. No facial embeddings or feature vectors are stored to disk.

## 6. Real-World Environmental Challenges

| Environmental Stressor | Impact on Facial Vision | EON Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Low Illumination ($<50\text{ lux}$)** | Image underexposure, sensor noise, lost edge contours. | Auto-exposure compensation; fallback to acoustic and skeletal modalities. |
| **Severe Backlighting** | Facial region silhouetted against bright windows. | Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) applied to face crop. |
| **Head Pose Yaw ($>45^\circ$)** | Profile view occludes half the facial landmarks. | Head pose monitoring triggers automatic uncertainty penalty; flags profile mode. |
| **Face Masks / Hand Coverings** | Mouth and nose completely occluded. | MediaPipe landmark confidence gating; automatic fallback to upper-face AUs (AU1, AU2, AU4, AU5, AU6, AU7) with increased epistemic uncertainty. |
| **Robot Kinematic Vibration** | Motion blur during robot wheel or bipedal locomotion. | Inter-frame temporal averaging; dynamic dropping of blurred frames via Laplacian sharpness filter. |
| **Demographic Fairness** | Disparate accuracy across Fitzpatrick skin tones (I–VI). | Training evaluation benchmarks across diverse skin types; continuous monitoring of calibration error. |

*Critical Legal Analysis:* Commercial robotics deployments cannot ship models trained directly on AffectNet, RAF-DB, CK+, or DISFA without exposing the organization to significant intellectual property infringement liabilities. EON's commercial strategy requires:
1. Utilizing pretrained foundation vision models with permissive commercial licenses (Google MediaPipe under Apache 2.0).
2. Training downstream regression heads on permissively licensed data (e.g., synthetic datasets generated via Blender/Unity, or contracted crowdsourced datasets with explicit commercial assignment).

## 9. Privacy, Ethics, and Regulatory Compliance

1. **No Facial Recognition / Biometrics:** EON performs facial *expression analysis*, not facial *identification*. No persistent facial templates, embeddings, or identity classifiers are generated or retained.
2. **GDPR Compliance (Article 9):** Biometric data used for uniquely identifying a natural person is prohibited without explicit consent. Because EON's ephemeral tracking cannot uniquely identify individuals across sessions, it minimizes regulatory classification as high-risk biometric identification.
3. **EU AI Act Alignment (2024 [SRC-050]):** EON includes system configuration flags allowing operators to disable facial affect classification in jurisdictions where workplace emotion analysis is prohibited.
4. **Safety Controller Isolation:** Facial expression outputs cannot alter robot motion safety corridors or trigger emergency stops (`is_authoritative_stop=False`).

## 10. EON-Specific Recommendations

1. **Adopt Two-Stage Observation Architecture:** Output explicit Action Unit intensities alongside projected VAD coordinates.
2. **Deploy Clean-Room Apache 2.0 Vision Stack:** Pair SCRFD-500M with MediaPipe Face Mesh to ensure absolute commercial safety.
3. **Calibrate Facial Confidence:** Scale softmax/regression outputs using Temperature Scaling and penalize confidence linearly when face yaw exceeds $\pm 30^\circ$.
4. **Handle Occlusion Gracefully:** When lower facial landmarks are occluded by masks or hands, zero out lip AUs and report elevated epistemic uncertainty.
