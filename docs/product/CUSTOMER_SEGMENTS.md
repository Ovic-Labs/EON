# 1. Purpose

This document defines the customer segments, target industries, buyer personas, customer needs and market-entry priorities for OVIC EON.

OVIC EON is an emotional and social intelligence infrastructure platform designed for robotics companies, developers, integrators and research organizations.

The product provides modular capabilities for:

- Context-aware reasoning.
- Personality simulation.

# 2. Product Positioning

## 2.1 Product Definition

OVIC EON is a modular emotional intelligence infrastructure platform for robotics.

It enables robotics manufacturers and developers to integrate emotional, social and behavioral intelligence capabilities into robotic systems through reusable software engines, APIs, SDKs and robotics integration interfaces.

OVIC EON is not intended to replace:

- Robot motion controllers.
- Hardware safety systems.
- Emergency-stop systems.
- Certified industrial safety controllers.
- General-purpose robotic operating systems.
- Manufacturer-specific control software.

Instead, it provides a complementary intelligence layer that can work alongside existing robotics architectures.

## 2.2 Core Value Proposition

**OVIC EON helps robotics companies build robots that can interpret social context, manage interactions, maintain appropriate interaction memory and generate context-aware responses.**

Its value comes from offering reusable intelligence infrastructure instead of requiring every robotics company to independently develop all seven intelligence capabilities.

- 100% edge-local processing (air-gapped operation without cloud dependency).
- Deterministic memory lifecycle with cryptographically secure data erasure.
- Deterministic failure isolation preventing software lockups.
- ROS 2 and lightweight Python/C++ interfaces.
- Strictly bounded CPU utilization to preserve compute for robot safety navigation.

### Integration Expectations

- Plug-and-play integration with standard assistive robot sensory inputs.
- Compatible with institutional air-gapped hospital networks.
- Comprehensive documentation of uncertainty handling and failure modes.

### Commercial Expectations

- Extended procurement and evaluation cycles (6–18 months).

### Deployment Constraints

- Must operate reliably without internet connectivity.
- Must execute on edge compute enclosures with strict thermal limits.

### Security and Privacy Expectations

- Compliance with HIPAA security rules and GDPR special-category health data requirements.
- Strictly zero transmission or unencrypted retention of raw facial video, voice audio, or biometric embeddings.
- Role-based access control and tamper-evident local audit logging.

### Product Limitations and Regulatory Boundary

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Non-Medical Device Notice:** OVIC EON is **NOT** a certified medical device under US FDA regulations (21 CFR), European Union Medical Device Regulation (EU MDR 2017/745), or international equivalent standards. OVIC EON must **NEVER** be used for medical diagnosis, psychiatric triage, clinical depression/anxiety scoring, suicide risk assessment, or physical therapy authorization. It is exclusively an interaction-support middleware platform.

# 5. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

The Ideal Customer Profile describes the type of organization OVIC EON should prioritize during its initial commercial development.

## 5.1 Primary ICP

A robotics company that:

- Develops humanoid, service or social robots.
- Builds products that interact directly with humans.
- Has an engineering or AI development team.
- Uses Python, C++, ROS 2 or comparable software infrastructure.
- Needs reusable intelligence capabilities.
- Has an active robotics development program.
- Can evaluate and integrate third-party software.
- Has a defined product roadmap.
- Has a technical decision-maker available.
- Has a budget for software licensing or engineering partnerships.

## 5.2 Secondary ICP

A robotics integrator or software company that:

- Works with multiple robotics manufacturers.
- Integrates perception and AI software.
- Builds ROS 2-based systems or equivalent middleware.
- Needs reusable software components.
- Has recurring customer integration projects.
- Can include OVIC EON in its delivery architecture.

## 5.3 Disqualification Criteria

An organization may not be a suitable initial customer if:

- It has no active robotics project.
- It has no relevant human-interaction requirement.
- It cannot support third-party integration.
- It requires unsupported hardware dependencies.
- It requires unsafe or unlawful behavior.
- It requires unsupported production guarantees.
- It expects unverified performance or accuracy claims.
- Its requirements exceed the available engineering and support capacity.

| Commercial readiness | Is there a purchasing or evaluation budget? |
| Decision-maker access | Can the technical buyer be reached? |
| Integration opportunity | Is a pilot or evaluation feasible? |
| Strategic value | Could the account become a reference customer? |

Suggested account priority:

- **Tier 1:** Strong product fit, active project and identifiable technical buyer.
- **Tier 2:** Strong product fit but unclear timing or purchasing readiness.
- **Tier 3:** Potential future customer with limited immediate opportunity.

These tiers are internal prospecting classifications, not confirmed buying intent.

# 15. Strategic Risks

| Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Long OEM procurement cycles | Delayed revenue | Begin with smaller evaluation opportunities |
| Complex robotics integration | Increased engineering effort | Prioritize stable SDKs and APIs |
| Unclear customer demand | Product-market mismatch | Conduct customer discovery |
| Hardware diversity | Compatibility challenges | Define supported reference platforms |
| Privacy concerns | Customer rejection | Build privacy controls into the architecture |
| Safety concerns | Deployment restrictions | Maintain separation from safety-critical control |
| Competition from in-house systems | Reduced adoption | Demonstrate integration and development value |
| Unverified performance claims | Loss of trust | Publish measured benchmarks only |
| Limited engineering resources | Slow delivery | Prioritize core use cases and reusable components |

# Final Product Strategy Statement

OVIC EON will initially focus on robotics organizations whose products require meaningful human interaction, particularly humanoid, social, companion and service robotics.

The platform will expand through robotics software companies, system integrators, industrial robotics manufacturers and research institutions.

The long-term objective is to establish OVIC EON as reusable emotional and social intelligence infrastructure that robotics manufacturers can integrate into their own products.
